# Young Israel of Scarsdale — Synagogue — Physical Model & Representation

**Alexander Gorlin Architects** · Fall 2025 · Scarsdale, NY
Category: Professional Work
With: Alexander Gorlin Architects
Role: Physical model (lead builder), axonometric drawings, interior renderings

> At Alexander Gorlin Architects, I hand-built a 24 × 44″ HO-scale model of the synagogue and contributed axonometric drawings and interior renderings.

At Alexander Gorlin Architects, I worked on the Young Israel of Scarsdale synagogue project, where I was primarily responsible for constructing a 24 × 44″ HO-scale physical model. The model was entirely hand-built using foam core and related materials, and served as a key tool for design development and client communication.

The model was used to study overall massing, façade articulation, and the building’s relationship to its site at a scale that allowed both spatial clarity and material precision. In parallel, I contributed to axonometric drawings that clarified program organization, circulation, and spatial relationships across multiple levels, and assisted in producing interior renderings of public spaces, focusing on light, material continuity, and spatial proportion.

Together, the physical model, drawings, and renderings formed a cohesive representational workflow that reflects the collaborative and iterative nature of professional architectural practice.
